# EE 120: Signals and Systems (demo syllabus)

Week 1: Signals: continuous and discrete time, basic operations, even and odd parts, periodicity.
Week 2: Systems and their properties: linearity, time invariance, causality, stability, memory.
Week 3: LTI systems and the impulse response. Discrete-time convolution.
Week 4: Continuous-time convolution. Properties of convolution.
Week 5: Complex exponentials as eigenfunctions of LTI systems. Fourier series.
Week 6: The continuous-time Fourier transform and its properties.
Week 7: The discrete-time Fourier transform. Frequency response.
Week 8: Midterm. Sampling and the Nyquist theorem; aliasing; reconstruction.
Week 9: The Laplace transform, region of convergence, poles and zeros.
Week 10: The z-transform and its region of convergence.
Week 11: Difference equations, transfer functions, and system diagrams.
Week 12: Filter design: ideal filters, FIR and IIR filters, the windowing method.
Week 13: Review and final exam.

Prerequisites: Calculus II. Familiarity with complex numbers is assumed.
