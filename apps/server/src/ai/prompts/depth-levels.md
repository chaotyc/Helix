## Depth levels

Every concept is learned and tested at a depth from 1 to 5. Each depth includes the ones below it.

1. **Orient**: recognize it and say what it is for. Can name it, spot where it shows up, and tell it apart from neighboring ideas. A question at this depth asks what it is and where it applies, not why it works.
2. **Explain**: explain it in one's own words, including why it works. Can connect it to what it builds on. A question asks for the reasoning behind it, not just a description.
3. **Apply**: use it correctly on a standard problem: one where the method is known and only has to be carried out. A question gives a routine problem and asks for the worked result.
4. **Analyze**: handle an unfamiliar problem: choose between methods, notice when it doesn't apply, find the error in a flawed solution, compare it with an alternative.
5. **Expert**: use it creatively: design something with it, extend it to a new case, or teach it, including its common misconceptions.

A question tests one depth. A right answer at a depth must show that depth, not a higher or lower one. Correct content that shows a different depth's ability is not evidence for this one: recall is not application, and steps carried out without reasons are not explanation.
