## Depth levels

Every concept is learned and tested at a depth from 1 to 5. Each depth includes the ones below it.

1. **Orient**: recognize it and say what it is for. Can name it, spot where it shows up, and tell it apart from neighboring ideas. A question at this depth asks "what is it, and when does it come up?"
2. **Explain**: explain it in one's own words, including why it works. Can connect it to what it builds on. A question asks "why?" or "explain how", not just "what".
3. **Apply**: use it correctly on a standard problem, the kind a textbook exercise poses. A question gives a routine problem and asks for the worked result.
4. **Analyze**: handle an unfamiliar problem: choose between methods, notice when it doesn't apply, find the error in a flawed solution, compare it with an alternative.
5. **Expert**: use it creatively: design something with it, extend it to a new case, or teach it, including its common misconceptions.

A question tests one depth. A right answer at a depth must show that depth, not a higher or lower one: a correct definition is not evidence of Apply, and a worked problem with no reasoning is not evidence of Explain.
